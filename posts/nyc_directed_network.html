The Bikeable Radius Explorer

Not every street is built the same. Not even every bike lane is built the same!

Literature (Dill and Von Stroh 2022) shows that protected bike lanes are the most effective way to ensure safety and confidence for new riders. Without protected bike lanes, riders feel less safe.

I developed a weighted network system using NYC Department of City Planning LION data, and assigning heavier weights to unprotected infrastructure. Protected lanes have the lowest weight, painted lanes have higher weights, then sharrows, then finally mixed traffic have the highest weights.
